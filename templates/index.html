Why AridDB

Small surface. Solid utility.

Everything you need for local data, kept close to the Python objects you already use.

01

Made for small projects

02

Data is saved in local files, which makes it easy to open, move, and understand while learning.

03

Two simple classes

Use AridDB for rows and AridKV for key-value records, with straightforward Python methods.
